mac waters — multimedia composer, violist, and improviser

mac waters is a multimedia composer, violist, and improviser based in vermont. a graduate of the dartmouth college mfa in sonic practice, mac composes multichannel and electroacoustic music for concert, installation, and film settings, and works across interactive media and video, extended reality, improvisation, graphic design, and game design.

mac’s works have been performed by fonema consort, wet ink, mivos quartet, conrad tao, ostravska banda, and ars futura, and mac has performed as a violist-improviser with tomeka reid’s stringtet and anthony braxton’s creative orchestra.

BA Music & Medieval & Renaissance Studies, Columbia University. MFA Sonic Practice, Dartmouth College.

work: DreamVisions+, GUIDED_MEDITATIONS, TikTokBox, soloviolarecital, SHOKWII, and concert music including pop song, Justin Bieber — Baby (remix), and erosion as a form of creation.

DreamVisions+ [2025-ongoing]

a transdigital, transhistorical odyssey into the realm of fiction and truth, reality and virtuality. rooted in late medieval dream-vision poems (Chaucer’s House of Fame, Langland’s Piers Plowman, the Roman de la Rose), DreamVisions+ reads these psychedelic allegories as proto-VR and stages a multimedia dream vision of the digital age: an expansive intermedia universe spanning live performance, immersive projection, game, and installation. in DreamVisions+_WALLED_GARDEN.exe, visitors boot up a lost-to-time video game on a CRT, where an anthropomorphized rose explores a walled garden, conversing with Lady Reason, Diversion, and Idleness.

DreamVisions+ [2025], workshop performance, DREAM Studio, Dartmouth College

DreamVisions+_WALLED_GARDEN.exe [2026], interactive game installation, Off Hours, Nearburg Gallery, Hanover, NH

tiktokbox.txt

TikTokBox [2022/2024]

a live improvised performance that turns my TikTok For You Page into a well of audiovisual material. custom Max/MSP/Jitter patches distort my phone’s live feed across two ten-foot screens and a 13-channel spatial audio array, while a motion-sensing Arduino bodysuit lets my whole body, not just my scrolling thumb, play the feed. a duet between a user and their feed: the individual and the collective, the conscious and the subconscious.

TikTokBox (2024), live audiovisual performance, Dartmouth College New Music Festival, Hanover, NH

soloviolarecital.txt

soloviolarecital [2024]

a freely improvised viola solo, captured by a 16-camera OptiTrack motion capture system, becomes the animation for an avatar on a virtual soundstage. as the improvisation builds and the camera closes in, the digital underbelly surfaces: textures dissolve to a grey humanoid model, the skeleton pokes through, fingers fall out of sync, the bow pierces the instrument. then the sound stops, and the video snaps to the real me, making eye contact with the camera.

soloviolarecital (2024), motion capture video, DREAM Studio, Dartmouth College

SHOKWII.txt

SHOKWII [2023]

locked behind a decade-old password is an archive of videos made by a hyperactive ten-year-old with an internet connection and a webcam: Shokwii101, the YouTube channel I hosted in 2010. SHOKWII collages, distorts, and remixes that archive, using YouTube itself as an instrument, into a dance sequence, a housephone montage, a commercial break, and a collapse. built around failed virality, awkward silences, and low view counts, it is of me but not about me.

SHOKWII (2023), film (20 min), (A)TEMPORALITIES showcase, Hanover, NH

Concert Music

my scored concert works are playful, theatrical compositions that often provide spaces for sonic exploration between improvisation and strict notation, between electronic and acoustic sound worlds. i find myself interested in sonic memory, genre & pastiche, and underlying assumptions re: traditional classsical performance practice.
