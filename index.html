about me 💿🏰🎻

mac waters is a multimedia composer, violist, and improviser based in vermont. a graduate of the dartmouth college mfa in sonic practice, mac composes multichannel and electroacoustic music for concert, installation, and film settings, and works across interactive media and video, extended reality, improvisation, graphic design, and game design.

mac’s works have been performed by fonema consort, wet ink, mivos quartet, conrad tao, ostravska banda, and ars futura, and mac has performed as a violist-improviser with tomeka reid’s stringtet and anthony braxton’s creative orchestra.

at dartmouth college, mac is the digital technician specialist for the departments of film & media studies and studio art, and previously served as an XR for the humanities fellow at the DREAM Studio (dartmouth resources for emergent arts and media).

contact: macarthur.waters@gmail.com
